Our interview process

Four stages, usually completed within two weeks.

  1. Intro call (30 minutes). Motivation, and what you want to work on next.
  2. Technical deep dive (60 minutes). We walk through a system you have built. Expect questions on state management, rendering performance and how you tested it.
  3. Practical exercise (90 minutes, pairing). You extend a small React and TypeScript codebase with one of our engineers. We care about how you reason under uncertainty, not about memorised APIs.
  4. Team and values conversation (45 minutes). Safety culture, on-call ownership, and giving difficult feedback.

What we assess

  • Depth in one area rather than shallow breadth everywhere.
  • Whether you measure before you optimise.
  • Clear written communication - we are a documentation-heavy company.
