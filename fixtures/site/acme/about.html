About us

Founded in 2016 in Bristol, Acme Robotics automates material handling in warehouses. We employ 180 people across engineering, operations and support, and our fleet software coordinates more than 4,000 robots in production.

How we work

Small autonomous teams, trunk-based development, and a strong bias towards observability. Engineers own their services in production and take part in a shared on-call rota.

Our values

  • Safety first - our machines share floors with people.
  • Measure before optimising.
  • Write it down; decisions outlive conversations.
