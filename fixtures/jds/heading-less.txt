Acme Robotics is hiring a backend engineer for our fleet services team in
Bristol. You would be joining a group of six engineers who keep the coordination
layer running for several thousand robots, so we need someone who has spent at
least four years building production backend systems and who is comfortable
owning a service end to end. Day to day you would be designing and shipping HTTP
APIs in Node.js and TypeScript, tuning PostgreSQL queries that have started to
creak under load, and taking your turn on a shared on-call rota. We care a great
deal about people who can reason about distributed systems failure modes, since
a stalled robot is a stalled warehouse, and we expect you to write tests as a
matter of course rather than as an afterthought. It would be genuinely useful if
you had worked with Kubernetes before, and anyone who has touched ROS or another
robotics middleware will find plenty to be interested in here, though neither is
a dealbreaker. We would also love it if you enjoyed mentoring, because two of
the team are relatively junior. Compensation is competitive and we offer 28 days
of holiday.
